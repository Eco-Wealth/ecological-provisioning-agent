# Owner and Laborer Market

The kernel supports coordination between people who own or steward places and people who can perform useful work.

## Owner needs

- Clarify the real problem.
- Know the safest first action.
- Scope work small enough to start.
- Understand evidence needed.
- Avoid false claims.
- See maintenance implications.

## Laborer needs

- Understand task, location context, tools, materials, safety, and acceptance criteria.
- Know what proof to collect.
- Avoid ambiguous or unsafe assignments.
- Produce a receipt that improves future work.

## Market posture

The public kernel does not hire, pay, schedule, insure, certify, or dispatch. It prepares work packets and receipts that humans or downstream systems can review.
