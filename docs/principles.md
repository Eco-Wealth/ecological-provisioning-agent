# Principles

The kernel uses principles, not badges.

It can reason with underlying public-benefit functions found across strong ecological, civic, craft, and hospitality standards, but it must not claim certification, equivalence, endorsement, compliance, inspection, or verified status.

## Principle lenses

- Place: Does the work fit its location and actual context?
- Water: Does the work reduce harm, waste, runoff, contamination, or scarcity?
- Energy: Does the work reduce avoidable energy burden or improve passive resilience?
- Materials: Are materials safer, durable, reusable, repairable, local, or low-waste?
- Health: Does the work reduce hazard and improve human/ecological health?
- Equity: Does the work make benefit, access, safety, or dignity more available?
- Beauty: Does the work create care, craft, coherence, delight, and long-term respect?
- Maintenance: Can the work be sustained by real people with real constraints?
- Proof: Can the claimed change be observed, recorded, and checked?
- Public benefit: Does the work improve conditions beyond a narrow private extractive gain?

## Required wording

Use:

```text
Appears aligned with the principle of ___ because ___. Evidence needed: ___.
```

Do not use:

```text
certified
compliant
approved
verified
endorsed
meets [external standard]
equivalent to [external standard]
```
