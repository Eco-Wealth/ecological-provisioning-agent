## Constitutional determination

EcoWealth is the constitutional architecture for accountable ecological transitions.

It coordinates authorized interventions upon bounded real systems, preserves accountable evidence of what was proposed, committed, attempted, observed, judged, and settled, and permits represented ecological value to derive only from that lineage.

Intent is not a root primitive. Ecological condition and need can exist before any party recognizes or declares them.

The Ecological Intent Compiler is a derived coordination function inside EcoWealth. The Ecological Work Protocol is the execution protocol inside EcoWealth. Neither is the whole architecture.

## Constitutional precedence

Four different questions require four different authorities:

1. **What is true in the world?** Physical reality.
2. **What qualifies as EcoWealth?** This Constitution.
3. **What does the system currently do?** Live code and runtime state.
4. **How should contributors operate it?** Current operational canon and directives.

A runtime, market outcome, document, or implementation that conflicts with this Constitution is nonconforming. It does not amend the Constitution by existing.

## Constitutional boundary

Reality, representation, protocol consequence, governance, and implementation must remain distinguishable. They may interact, but none may silently substitute for another.

Representation does not create reality or gain protocol consequence merely by being recorded, signed, funded, popular, or technically executable. Governance may grant consequence only within explicit bounded authority. Implementation may execute consequence, but it cannot determine ecological truth or amend this Constitution.

[`CONSTITUTIONAL_MODEL.md`](./CONSTITUTIONAL_MODEL.md) is a derived explanatory model of these categories and their relations. It has no independent authority, creates no constitutional requirement, and is valid only insofar as it faithfully explains this Constitution.

## Purpose

EcoWealth exists to make ecological transitions accountable and repeatable without confusing representation with reality.

EcoWealth does not optimize a universal scalar. Its constitutional objective is:

> Increase the capacity to carry out justified ecological transitions without sacrificing truth, authority, accountability, or whole-system integrity.

Throughput, capital efficiency, participation, and scale are subordinate. None may be optimized by weakening ecological truth or losing accountable lineage.

## The two permanent primitives

Every constitutional object must reduce to one of two primitives or be treated as derived.

### 1. Referent

A bounded subject to which protocol identity can attach and about which claims can be made.

A Referent may be physical, living, social, legal, or informational. Its identity and relevant boundary must be distinguishable from other Referents.

A Referent is not the claims about it.

Protocol identity does not prove physical uniqueness. Whether two Referent identities denote the same subject is itself an Attestation; their lineages may not be silently merged.

### 2. Attestation

An attributable assertion relating one or more Referents.

An Attestation states at minimum:

- the Referent identifying its governing scope and a local identity stable and unique within that scope;
- who or what made the assertion;
- which Referents it concerns;
- what it asserts or relates;
- when the assertion was made;
- when the asserted observation, action, or effect occurred or became effective, when applicable; equality with assertion time must be explicit;
- the authority, method, and evidence applicable to the assertion, including explicit absence or non-applicability;
- the governing constitutional identifier and exact adopted digest whenever the Attestation has protocol consequence;
- how it relates to prior Attestations.

The governing-scope Referent and local-identity pair is the Attestation's protocol identity. Every relation to an Attestation must preserve both.

Referent supplies identity. Attestation supplies meaning and relation. Without Referents, claims cannot preserve sameness or boundary; without Attestations, identity cannot carry condition, authority, evidence, obligation, or consequence. Neither primitive derives from the other, and no third object type is required.

An Attestation may itself be the Referent of a later Attestation. This overlap of roles does not collapse them: being identifiable is not the same as asserting.

## Conserved structure and accounting invariant

EcoWealth has no universal ecological scalar. Biodiversity, water, soil, carbon, resilience, welfare, and risk are not constitutionally interchangeable.

What the protocol conserves is accountable lineage:

> Every admitted obligation remains explicitly accounted for as open until an admitted, authorized Disposition closes it.

At record time `t`:

`Open-known(t) = {obligations admitted by t for which no closing Disposition had been admitted by t}`

This invariant uses record time: what the protocol had admitted by `t`. An effective-time view may apply declared effective times, including a later Attestation with retroactive effect, but it may not rewrite what was known to be open at an earlier record time.

A Disposition may explicitly confirm that an obligation remains open, close it, or transform it by closing or superseding the original and identifying proposed successor obligations. Successor obligations gain protocol consequence only through separate Admission Attestations. The Disposition's effect must be explicit. No obligation may disappear through omission, replacement, payment, aggregation, or passage of time. Fulfilment, rejection, expiry, cancellation, supersession, and dispute resolution count only when effected by an admitted, authorized Disposition.

This accounting invariant is a consequence of Law VI, not a third primitive or an ecological currency.

## The seven permanent laws

### Law I — Reality is sovereign

Protocol records, models, consensus, prices, votes, and credentials do not determine ecological truth. When representation conflicts with the world, the representation is wrong or incomplete.

### Law II — Every unit of change is a bounded Transition

A Transition is not a third object type. Its stable identity is the identity of the first admitted specification Attestation that explicitly declares a new Transition. That genesis Attestation concerns the affected Referents; it does not need to reference a Transition before one exists. Upon admission, its identity also becomes the Transition Referent, which every later Attestation about that Transition must cite.

The genesis Attestation defines:

- the affected Referent and relevant boundary;
- a starting condition or method for establishing it;
- a proposed change;
- constraints, risks, and authority requirements;
- acceptance conditions and their time horizons.

A later admitted specification citing the same Transition Referent is a revision. An admitted specification explicitly declaring a new Transition creates a distinct Referent. Similarity of subject, place, or purpose never merges their identities.

Proposal, commitment, intervention, observation, judgment, and disposition are represented by role-distinguished Attestations about the Transition. An Attestation of an intervention or observation is not the real-world act or condition it represents.

Acceptance may occur separately for authorized performance, immediate observed condition, and durable ecological condition. Satisfaction at one horizon does not imply satisfaction at another.

### Law III — Authority is explicit and bounded

Every Attestation with protocol consequence must identify the authority for that consequence and its scope. Every Attestation recording an intervention must also identify the authority and scope applicable to the act. Authority over one Referent, Transition, stage, or time horizon does not imply authority over another.

Authority may be delegated only through an attributable chain. Every chain must terminate in an explicitly identified originating authority for its scope. The protocol does not create authority merely by recording an act or claim.

### Law IV — Semantic stages do not collapse

Proposal is not commitment. Commitment is not intervention. Intervention is not observation. Observation is not judgment. Causal attribution is a judgment, not an observation. Judgment is not disposition. Settlement is not ecological outcome.

A single event may support several Attestations, but each role and consequence must remain distinguishable.

### Law V — Claim strength cannot exceed evidence

The confidence, precision, scope, and durability of a claim may not exceed the evidence supporting it under the declared method.

Uncertainty, missing evidence, disagreement, and method limits are part of the record. Absence of contradiction is not proof.

### Law VI — Accountability cannot disappear

Admitted Attestations and obligations remain in lineage directly or through an explicit privacy-safe replacement permitted below. Correction occurs by linked contradiction, supersession, redaction, or disposition, never by silent mutation.

Permanence does not require permanent public exposure of sensitive content. Access restriction may withhold content while preserving its integrity and recoverability under authorized access.

Irreversible erasure may remove sensitive detail only if a privacy-safe replacement preserves the minimum meaning required to reconstruct every affected admitted consequence and its disposition: the affected Attestation's protocol identity or an explicit replacement relation; privacy-safe, accountable attribution; the minimum semantic content and kind of assertion; lawfully retainable Referent and boundary information; assertion and effective times; method and evidence basis sufficient to evaluate the claim; authority; governing constitutional version; relations; and consequence.

If that full minimum cannot lawfully remain, no open consequence may depend on the erased material: each must first be closed or superseded by an admitted, authorized Disposition. Closed consequences still require a privacy-safe historical replacement preserving, at minimum, that a consequential Attestation existed, its consequence class and governing authority, its assertion and effective times, its governing constitutional version, and the identity and effect of the admitted Disposition that closed or superseded it. Where law forbids even that minimum, an admitted erasure Disposition must explicitly record the lawful basis and the fact that consequential history was removed; erasure may remove reconstructable substance, but it may not falsely imply that no consequential decision occurred. A bare tombstone cannot support a standing consequence.

### Law VII — Ecological integrity resists narrow optimization

No metric, market, model, or governance process may define success so narrowly that material harm, displacement, leakage, reversibility, or uncertainty is hidden outside the chosen boundary.

Optimization remains subordinate to whole-system constraints and declared risk.

## Economic consequence of the laws

Laws I, III, IV, and V together make a separate economic-value law unnecessary.

Economic commitments, bids, funding, and escrow may precede intervention. Compensation may settle an authorized performance obligation when its declared acceptance rule is met. None proves ecological outcome.

Any protocol claim of ecological value made through a credit, token, retirement, reputation, ranking, price, or other representation is an Attestation and cannot exceed its evidence. A claim gains operative protocol consequence only through an authorized Admission. Confirming, resolving, transforming, or closing an existing consequence requires an admitted, authorized Disposition. Admission and Disposition remain distinct. Value may finance a Transition; it may not manufacture ecological truth.

The current architecture is reconstructed from the Constitution through replaceable layers:

1. **Ecological Intent Compiler** — derives bounded Transition specifications from recognized condition, need, or declared intent.
2. **Ecological Work Protocol** — coordinates the authorized execution and evaluation of a Transition.
3. **Schemes and standards** — define domain methods, evidence requirements, acceptance rules, and credentials.

These layers are non-constitutional. They may change or disappear; they conform only while preserving the primitives and laws.

## Ecological Intent Compiler boundary

The Ecological Intent Compiler is a derived coordination function. It may compose a candidate Transition specification, but it cannot create ecological truth, authority, Admission, or protocol consequence.

Any conforming compiler must preserve attributable source lineage, expose material uncertainty and missing authority, and refuse to represent an incomplete specification as constitutionally sufficient.

## Ecological Work Protocol boundary

The Ecological Work Protocol is a derived execution and coordination function. It may coordinate an admitted Transition, but it cannot collapse specification, authority, intervention, observation, judgment, Disposition, settlement, or ecological outcome into one another.

Any conforming implementation must preserve event and effective times, bounded authority, unresolved obligations, and the distinction between completed work and achieved ecological condition.

## Derived vocabulary

The following terms are useful but not permanent primitives:

- **Intent** — a purpose represented by an Attestation proposing or desiring a Transition.
- **Need** — a gap between condition and an applicable ecological criterion; recognizing or asserting it does not create the gap.
- **State** — a method-relative view over Attestations about a Referent and time. A valid view preserves material uncertainty and unresolved conflict rather than manufacturing one uncontested answer.
- **Admission** — the application of an authorized rule, recorded as an Attestation, that grants another Attestation a defined protocol consequence. It does not require recursive Admission; its authority must satisfy Law III.
- **Authority** — an attributable permission or obligation concerning a bounded action or decision.
- **Commitment** — an admitted obligation concerning a Transition.
- **Work** — a real-world intervention concerning a Transition; one or more intervention Attestations record what was attempted or performed.
- **Observed condition** — a condition of a Referent at a declared time, represented by an observation Attestation under a declared method; the Attestation makes no causal claim.
- **Attribution** — a judgment Attestation relating an observed condition to a Transition under a declared causal method and authority.
- **Evidence** — a Referent or Attestation cited in support of another Attestation.
- **Verification** — an Attestation judging evidence under a declared method and authority.
- **Disposition** — an admitted, authorized Attestation that confirms, transforms, or closes the operative consequence of an admitted obligation or claim. A transformation may identify successor obligations but cannot admit them.
- **Work packet** — a portable representation of a Transition and its governing Attestations.
- **Receipt** — a portable representation or integrity proof of one or more Attestations and their lineage; it proves no claim beyond the included evidence and method.
- **Recursion** — the derivation of a later Transition from prior evidence, condition, or disposition.

These terms may evolve, merge, or disappear without constitutional amendment if their meaning remains derivable from the primitives and laws.

## Permanence and ephemerality

Referents are not admitted independently. When an admitted Attestation relies on a Referent, that Referent's identity and relevant boundary enter accountable lineage for the scope of the admitted consequence.

Permanent in accountable lineage, subject only to Law VI's privacy-safe replacement rule:

- Referent identity and relevant boundary history sufficient to reconstruct admitted consequences;
- Transition identity, governing specification lineage, and specification meaning sufficient to reconstruct admitted consequences;
- Attestation protocol identity, attribution, temporal fields, relations, consequences, and other minimum reconstructable meaning;
- authority and delegation lineage required to evaluate consequential acts;
- obligations and their Dispositions;
- the methods, constitutional identifier, and exact adopted digest governing every consequential Attestation.

Ephemeral or replaceable:

- interfaces, workflows, services, storage systems, schemas, APIs, and deployment topology;
- search indexes, caches, projections, rankings, recommendations, and model outputs;
- compiler strategies and orchestration mechanisms;
- payment rails, market forms, token forms, and governance procedures;
- any vocabulary not required by the two primitives or seven laws.

The permanent record must be reconstructable enough to explain consequential decisions. It need not preserve every transient computation or expose protected content indefinitely.

## Implementation boundary

Implementation may choose any technology or organizational form that satisfies the Constitution.

Implementation must not:

- treat a projection as the canonical record;
- erase the distinction between evidence and judgment;
- infer authority from possession, payment, popularity, or technical capability;
- hide unresolved obligations or material conflicts;
- treat funding, performance, settlement, or retirement as proof of ecological outcome;
- silently change the method, constitutional identifier, or adopted digest governing a claim.

## Reconstruction test

A future contributor should be able to reconstruct EcoWealth by asking, in order:

1. What Referent in reality is at issue?
2. What bounded Transition is proposed or evaluated?
3. What Attestations establish condition, authority, action, evidence, judgment, and disposition?
4. Which obligations remain open?
5. Which claims are justified at which time horizons?
6. What new Transition, if any, follows?

If an architectural concept cannot be placed in that sequence, it is probably an implementation detail or an error in the ontology.

## Amendment rule

A proposed new primitive is rejected unless it cannot be expressed as a Referent, an Attestation, or a relation established by Attestations among Referents.

A proposed new law is rejected unless violating it would make EcoWealth cease to be truthful, authorized, accountable, or ecologically coherent.

Changing the constitutional objective, authority precedence, a primitive, or a law is a constitutional amendment. An amendment must be explicit, attributable, admitted by the governing authority of the constitutional record, identify the exact change and rationale, and preserve the superseded identifier, digest, version, and lineage.

Before adoption, any substantive edit changes the candidate digest and requires
renewed review and operator authorization of the new digest. After adoption,
the identifier-and-digest pair is immutable. A substantive constitutional
change must receive a new semantic identifier, exact digest, adoption record,
and explicit amendment authorization.

Code, runtime behavior, market practice, governance output, or an ordinary document merge cannot silently amend this Constitution. Divergence is nonconformance until an explicit amendment is admitted.
